### 👩‍💻 About Me

🎓 **B.Sc. Graduate in Computer Science & Engineering** with a strong focus on **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Natural Language Processing**.

My academic journey has equipped me with a solid theoretical foundation and hands-on experience in building predictive models, fine-tuning transformers, and analyzing complex datasets.

Beyond academia, I have developed a keen interest in **software engineering — particularly Java and Spring Boot**. I believe the future of AI lies in its *deployment*, so I am actively learning how to build production-grade, scalable systems that bridge the gap between research and real-world applications.

* 🔭 **Current Focus:** Fine-tuning transformer models, NLP pipelines, and end-to-end ML workflows.
* 🌱 **Expanding Horizons:** Generative AI, model deployment, and scalable backend architecture.
* ☕ **Engineering Core:** Java, Spring Boot RESTful APIs, SQL, and system design fundamentals.
* 🎯 **Looking For:** Entry-level opportunities where I can contribute to impactful projects while growing as a researcher and engineer.
* 🤝 **Collaboration:** Open to research collaborations, open-source contributions, and internships!
